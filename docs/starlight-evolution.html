How Evolution works

Gather duplicate extras, pick a card below, and confirm Evolve. Your card upgrades to the next Radiance frame.

  1. Collect duplicate copies beyond your protected first copy.
  2. Open a card in Ready to evolve or Your evolved cards.
  3. Confirm Evolve — extras are spent and the Radiance frame upgrades.
  4. Unfuse later if you want a partial refund of spent extras.
